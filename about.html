About Urban Gang Tour

We are the day
students never forget.

Urban Gang Tour started with a simple question: what happens when you stop telling young people what to do and start giving them a stage? What happens is exactly what you would expect. They rise to it.

The full picture

A movement.
Not a show.

Built by Eugine Micah and Lucy Ogunde, Urban Gang Tour is a registered Kenyan events company that produces full-scale talent, mentorship and entertainment programmes for high schools, universities, colleges, and campus communities across the country.

Every event is broadcast on Urban News on PPP TV Kenya, where the programme has reached up to 8 million viewers nationally. From Potential to Purpose is not a tagline. It is the lens through which every part of what we do is designed.

Students compete. Working professionals mentor. The crowd sings along. Trees go in the ground. Awards get handed out on stage. Then it all airs on national television. That is the whole brief.

Mission

"Find Kenyan young people with real talent and give them a real stage."

That is the whole mission. No filler. No participation certificates. A proper platform for real talent, broadcast to millions.

Vision

"A Kenya where no talented young person goes unseen."

Every county. Every school type. Every talent category. If you have something real, Urban Gang Tour will find you a stage.

The story

Where it started.

"What happens when you stop telling young people what to do and start giving them a stage? They rise to it. Every single time.
Eugine Micah · Co-Founder & Lead Host · Urban News, PPP TV Kenya

Eugine Micah is the face of Urban News on PPP TV Kenya, a daily programme with a national reach. He designs the format, books the talent, builds the partnerships, and holds the stage with the kind of energy that makes students lean in. Lucy Ogunde is the executive producer and co-host - the precision of someone who has thought through every detail and the warmth of someone who genuinely loves being in a room full of young people finding their voice. Together, they are the face and the engine of everything Urban Gang Tour produces.

Objectives

What this is
working toward.

01
Find what is already there. Every classroom in Kenya is hiding a future artist, journalist, model, or leader. We go in, build a stage, and let them be seen.
02
Pair ambition with access. Aspiring journalists with our camera crew. Future stylists with our modelling team. Young presenters on stage with Eugine and Lucy. The career a student dreams about is standing in front of them for a day.
03
Have real conversations. Mentorship that goes beyond the syllabus. Drug awareness with NACADA facilitators who speak how students actually speak. Real people. Real answers.
04
Eat, plant, smile, share. A meal with the students. Trees with Delo Greens Movement. Group photos. Urban Gang Moments instant prints. The kind of day they describe to their parents at dinner.
05
Put it on national television. The next day, the edit starts. Within weeks, their school is on Urban News on PPP TV Kenya. Up to 8 million viewers. A national audience to a student who never had one.
