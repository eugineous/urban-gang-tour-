Official Drug Prevention Partner

NACADA

The National Authority for the Campaign Against Drug Abuse does not just issue warnings. NACADA has certified facilitators who go into communities, speak the truth, and do the kind of work that changes the choices young people make before those choices change them.

Urban Gang Tour made them our Official Drug Prevention Partner because we refuse to run an event that is only about entertainment. At every school stop, a dedicated NACADA session runs inside the programme, led by facilitators who speak to students in the language students actually use. Real conversations. Real stories. Not a lecture.

"This partnership is active. It is confirmed across our 2026 school calendar. And it is one of the things that makes Urban Gang Tour something more than a show."
Official Broadcast Partner

PPP TV Kenya

Everything that happens at a UGT event is meant to be seen by more than the people in that field. Urban News on PPP TV Kenya is the broadcast engine that carries the stories of students on our stage to up to 8 million viewers nationally.

Eugine Micah and Lucy Ogunde are the faces of Urban News. Every school visit becomes a full TV episode. Every winner gets a national moment. Every institution gets seen by an audience that most schools will never reach on their own.

"PPP TV is not just where we broadcast. It is where we prove that what happened that day was real."
  • ReachUp to 8M viewers per episode, nationally
  • FormatFull TV episode per school visit
  • HostsEugine Micah & Lucy Ogunde
  • StatusActive broadcast - confirmed 2026

Modelling and Wellness Partner

Synapse Models

Founded by Esther Wambui Gakunju, Synapse Models is the organisation that turned modelling at Urban Gang Tour from a category into a conversation. Esther leads every modelling activity at every event, from the pre-event orientation to the runway itself, and brings a standard of professionalism to a school environment that students feel the moment she walks in.

But Synapse Models does something beyond the runway. Esther's presence at every event is also a wellness anchor. She is the person students gravitate towards when they need a real conversation, not a performance.

"That combination of grace and substance is exactly what this programme needed."
Entertainment Partner

The Experience Hub

The Experience Hub is the 30-person entertainment unit led by Hype Ola that turns the crowd at every Urban Gang Tour event into a participant rather than an audience. Three live hype sets per event. DJ Xavi on the decks. Khloe Nyarangi and five dancers on the floor. Hype Jay and Mercie Smalls on the mics.

This is not a playlist on shuffle. This is a live entertainment operation built specifically for the energy of a school or campus crowd.

"Between battles, after winners are announced, during the lunch hour. Wherever the energy could dip, The Experience Hub is already there."

Official Environmental Partner

Delo Greens
Movement

Delo Greens Movement is a community-driven urban environmental organisation founded and led by Justus Abuko, known to the Urban Gang Tour family as King TAE, The African Eagle. DGM exists to re-green city spaces, fight the creep of climate change, and build a generation of young Kenyans who treat the earth the way it deserves to be treated.

When Urban Gang Tour plants a tree at the end of an event day, it is not a photo opportunity. It is DGM's work, done through our platform, at a scale that grows with every school we visit.

"By the time a student who planted a tree with us is sitting their final exams, that tree will already be standing."
  • Goal10,000 schools across East & Central Africa
  • Reach50 campuses in 5 years
  • LeadJustus Abuko (King TAE)
  • StatusActive at every UGT event
Official Stage Audio Partner

Ashton Sounds

The stage sounds the way it does because of Ashton Sounds. Every PA system at every Urban Gang Tour event is their responsibility. Every microphone, every speaker stack, every clean transition between acts is the result of a team that takes live audio personally.

Ashton Sounds is headed by Jeff Ashton, CEO, who runs the technical operation alongside Fally Mark's Sauti Moto. That standard, behind every cable run and every monitor mix, is why the production sounds as serious as it looks.

"Audio is not what you notice when it is right. It is what you notice when it is wrong. Ashton makes sure you never notice."

Medical & Emergency Standby
Medical and Emergency Response Partner

Moyo Response

When hundreds or thousands of young people gather in one place, being prepared for the unexpected is not optional. Moyo Response is our medical and emergency response partner, ensuring that trained, equipped first-response capacity is present at every Urban Gang Tour event.

Their presence at our events means students, parents, and schools can relax. And it means that on the one day a year where a student stands in front of their entire school and takes the biggest risk of their year so far, the people around them are ready for anything.

"Safety is not a feature. It is a precondition."
Campus Partner

Hewitt and Bennet
International College

Hewitt and Bennet International College is a Kenya-based institution known for practical, career-focused training across healthcare, hospitality, and technical fields, with campuses in Nairobi CBD, Buruburu, and Thika. Their programmes are NITA-certified and built around guaranteed internship and attachment for every student, with a strong pipeline into international placements across the UK, Canada, the USA, and Europe.

Hewitt and Bennet joined the Urban Gang Tour family as a campus partner because they understand something most institutions take years to figure out: that confidence, self-expression, and the ability to hold a room are not soft skills. They are career skills.

"The students they train go out into the world and represent Kenya in healthcare facilities, hotel lobbies, and aircraft cabins across the globe. UGT gives those same students a stage before they get there."

Campus Partner

TIBS College

TIBS College is one of Kenya's active business and technical training institutions and an important part of Urban Gang Tour's expansion into higher education. Their students are exactly the kind of young people the tour was built for: ambitious, industry-focused, and ready to compete.

The partnership brings Urban Gang Tour into a campus environment where the audience is older, the conversations in the mentorship sessions go deeper, and the talent on stage has already started thinking seriously about what they want to build with their lives.

"TIBS is part of how Urban Gang Tour grows beyond the school gate."
