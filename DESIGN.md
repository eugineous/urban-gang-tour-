# Urban Gang Tour Design System

## Visual world

**Kenya, live and in frame.** A cinematic editorial event universe built from the tour's documentary photography, saturated magenta, electric cyan, signal yellow and deep ink. It feels like a confident global culture publisher: generous type, deliberate asymmetry, bold image crops and a single continuous journey—not a template, dashboard, or brochure.

## System tokens

- Ink: `#111111`
- Magenta: `#E6218C`
- Electric cyan: `#21C7E6`
- Signal yellow: `#FFD400`
- Paper: `#F8F4EC`
- Display: Anton; utility/body: Space Grotesk / Archivo.
- Shape: one confident frame system (1px ink rules, 16px radii, soft offset depth), never generic glass cards or repeated boxed grids.

## Cross-route rules

- Every public route uses one React-rendered surface; captured V25 HTML and its secondary runtime are retired from visitor-facing paths.
- Phone routes use one app shell: a visible five-item bottom navigation, a raised booking action, safe-area padding and obvious navigation continuity.
- Every public route has a deliberate task-specific composition while sharing the same nav, action and type system.
- The public people story names only the two founders: Eugine Micah and Lucy Ogunde.
- Real event, crew, gallery, product and video assets are preferred to decorative placeholders.
- Motion is purposeful: route/load feedback, raised CTA interaction, sheet transitions and media reveal. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Quality floor

Every public route must have a reachable route URL, content visible at phone width, legible type, labelled vector controls, focus treatment and enough bottom padding that fixed navigation cannot cover content.
