Control Room / creative review / round 01

Three jobs. Three visual voices.

These are candidate art directions for a more deliberate document ecosystem. The logo, information hierarchy, variable data, serials and QR codes stay in code; images are clean editorial backgrounds, never the source of operational information.

Review status — not final. The artwork is intentionally text-free so real names, dates, prices, consent language and verification can remain editable and accurate. These designs are evidence-informed hypotheses, not claims that a particular visual style is proven to raise sales.

What gets built after review

Each approved direction becomes a distinct reusable template family—not a single look stamped across every item.

  • Formal documents will prioritise legibility, factual input fields and a restrained logo placement.
  • Live-event assets will prioritise date, venue, access level, QR/serial verification and print-safe contrast.
  • Campaign assets will be designed as a system of poster, feed, story, ticket and landing-page variants, with real event data only.
  • Every final template receives a mobile preview, print check and an owner review before it replaces an existing default.
