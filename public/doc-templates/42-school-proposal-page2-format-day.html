This is not a talent show. It's a battle.

Talent doesn't just perform at Urban Gang Tour — it competes. Choir against choir. Dance crew against dance crew. Poet against poet. Back to back, until a clear winner is crowned in every category. And the one rule that runs the whole show: no competitor, no stage. If an act has nobody to battle, that slot stays closed. Every minute is earned, and the crowd stays locked in from the first drop to the crowning.
