THE POWER BEHIND THE TOUR
Urban Gang Tour
Urban Gang Tour is an events company founded by Eugine Micah and Lucy Ogunde, powered by its own crew and close creative partners — including Hype Ola of The Experience Hub, Jeff Ashton of Ashton Sounds, and Esther Wambui Gakunju of Synapse Models. The tour operates as its own company with its own rights, and everything it captures airs nationally through Urban News, the Urban News show with reach of up to 8 million viewers. tour camera crews are on the ground at every stop.
