The old site was one scrolling page. The new site is a hub with audience specific landing pages, because a principal, a brand manager, a dean of students, and a festival promoter all need to land on a page that speaks directly to them.

**Pages:**

**Navigation bar:** Home | About | The Experience | Book Us (dropdown: Schools, Universities and Colleges, Mega Events) | Partners | Impact | Tour Stops | Contact
**Sticky CTA button in nav, always visible:** BOOK THE TOUR (magenta)

**Design system (unchanged, non negotiable):**
- Dark first. Background #1A1A1A. The brand comes alive on dark.
- Primary Magenta #C7238E: headings, CTAs, links, highlights
- Secondary Orange #F5A623: subheadings, motto, warm emphasis, dividers
- White text on dark. Arial / sans serif only. No serif. No decorative fonts.
- Magenta to orange gradient bars as section dividers
- Scroll triggered fade ins, hover lifts on cards, smooth transitions
- Logo: 3D stylised URBAN (silver) / GANG (orange) / TOUR on magenta ribbon. Use logo_horizontal_2400x600.png in nav, square mark for favicon.
- Never on the website: registration number, internal pricing, internal costs, any church references or spiritual framing

# PAGE 2: ABOUT, WHO WE ARE

## Hero
**Headline:** WE TURN POTENTIAL INTO PURPOSE
**Sub:** Urban Gang Tour is a Kenyan youth talent search, mentorship, and awards concert programme. We move countrywide: high schools, universities, colleges, and large scale events.

## Our Story
Urban Gang Tour was born from a simple frustration: Kenya is overflowing with young talent that never gets a stage, a mentor, or a camera. So we built all three into one travelling machine. One day. One institution. Talent battles judged live, life skills pods that actually speak the language of young people, a modelling runway, an awards ceremony, and a broadcast on national TV through Urban News on PPP TV Kenya.

We are not a one off event company. We are a movement that keeps showing up.

## Mission
To discover, mentor, and platform young Kenyan talent through live events, life skills education, and national broadcast, turning raw potential into real purpose.

## Vision
To be Africa's leading youth talent and mentorship tour, where every young person, in every county, has a stage within reach.

## What Makes Us Different (5 differentiators)
1. **We come to you.** The full production travels: stage, sound, crew, cameras, hosts.
2. **Battles, not showcases.** Competitor vs competitor in 8 categories. Real stakes, real winners, real crowns.
3. **Mentorship built in.** Six Urban Pods run alongside the battles, covering money, digital skills, leadership, mental wellness, and substance abuse awareness with NACADA.
4. **National broadcast.** Every stop feeds Urban News on PPP TV Kenya plus short form content across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, and X.
5. **A real crew.** Around 30 trained crew members per event: hosts, DJs, stage managers, videographers, models, medics, and pod facilitators.

# PAGE 4: FOR SCHOOLS

## Hero
**Headline:** YOUR SCHOOL. THEIR STAGE.
**Sub:** A full day talent, mentorship, and awards takeover for your students, ending with a national TV feature.

## What Your School Gets
- A full production day: stage, sound, hosts, DJs, and an approximately 30 person crew
- 8 talent battle categories plus the modelling runway with 4 sub categories
- 6 Urban Pods covering talent, money, digital skills, leadership, mental wellness, and NACADA led substance abuse awareness
- The Mrs/Miss of the Institution crowning, presented by your Principal
- Full event videography and a feature on Urban News, PPP TV Kenya
- Social media coverage across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, and X
- Certificates for participants and winners

## How It Works (4 step strip)
1. **Reach out.** Tell us your school, student numbers, and preferred term dates.
2. **Recon visit.** Our team visits, meets your coordinators, and maps the venue.
3. **Build up.** We handle auditions guidance, programme, and production. You assign 1 to 2 staff coordinators and a few student assistants.
4. **Event day.** We arrive early, build the stage, run the day, crown your stars, and film everything.

## What We Ask From the School
- A field or hall, with access from early morning
- 1 to 2 staff coordinators and 3 to 4 student assistants
- Meals and refreshments for the visiting crew
- Confirmed event date and student participation

## Pricing
Every school is different, so every quotation is built for your numbers and your term calendar. Rates are negotiable and we work with schools of every size.
