Section one
The face
of the brand.

Eugine and Lucy steer every event from the first DJ set to the last award. That is not production value. That is identity.

Co-Founder
Eugine Micah
Creative Director · Lead Host · Urban News

There is a specific kind of person who can hold a thousand students' attention from a stage, manage a TV production, close a brand partnership and still be the last one to leave the venue. Eugine is that person. He built Urban Gang Tour as a vehicle for what he has always believed: that Kenyan youth do not need to be entertained, they need to be unleashed. The primary face of the brand both on screen and in the rooms where decisions get made.

@eugine.micah
Co-Founder
Lucy Ogunde
Executive Producer · Co-Host · Urban News

Lucy is the kind of person a room adjusts itself around. She co-hosts every event with Eugine, leads production strategy, drives the business forward, and has a deep instinct for what students respond to on and off stage. She built the operational backbone of Urban Gang Tour from the ground up. Every school that has met Lucy has called back.

@lucyogunde

The key partners.

The people who hold whole pillars of the production. Programme, sound, entertainment, modelling - each one carried by someone with a track record and a real stake in what we are building.

Programme Overseer · MC
MC Paps
Paps Nadhir Apapu

MC Paps can command any room and make every person in it feel like they were the one who mattered most. He oversees the full event programme, coordinates with school administration on the day, and brings a networks-first approach to building the brand's institutional relationships. Deeply invested in showing young Kenyans that the room they want to be in has always had space for them.

F

Production Partner
Fally Mark

The kind of partner who has already thought of the thing you forgot to plan for, and who shows up early regardless. Head of Entertainment
Hype Ola
Experience Hub lead

The Experience Hub - UGT's signature entertainment sequence - is his domain. He has built a team of creatives around him with one shared goal: no student in that crowd has a reason to look away from the stage.

Head of Modelling & Wellness
Esther Wambui Gakunju

Esther founded Synapse Models and brought that vision directly into Urban Gang Tour. The person students with questions go to and the person who ensures the programme leaves people feeling better than it found them.

03
Section three
Heads of departments.

Brand, content, decks, stage, environment, security. Six leads, six lanes, one production that runs because they cover the ground.

E
Brand Manager
Enock Wanyama

Enock lives at the intersection of identity and presentation. He manages the visual and strategic consistency of the Urban Gang Tour brand across every touchpoint, ensures every partner-facing document reflects the standard the brand sets, and keeps the look of everything intentional. He cares about getting it right because he knows the difference it makes.

Head of Social & Content
Rania Martin

Rania runs the content engine. On event day she is producing reels, coordinating with the camera crew, and building the social narrative that extends the event's reach from a school field to millions of feeds. Fast, precise, and with a genuine feel for what the audience responds to. Her work is how the world finds out what happened.

Head of DJs · Resident DJ
DJ Xavi

A television presenter and the resident DJ who sets the sonic tone for every event. He coordinates all DJ sets and handles every musical transition with the kind of reading of a room that only comes from years of doing it live. Responsible for making sure the crowd never has a reason to lose focus.

Head Stage Manager · Spoken Word Lead
Kalamu Nyeusi

Kalamu runs the stage with the calm authority of someone who has already thought through the twelve things that could go wrong. He manages programme flow, keeps time, and performs in the spoken word and poetry categories with genuine artistic conviction. He believes the stage is a sacred space - and it shows in how he manages it.

T
Head of Environment & Vendors
King TAE
The African Eagle

TAE oversees the green thread that runs through every Urban Gang Tour event. He founded and leads Delo Greens Movement, UGT's Official Environmental Partner, and brings that mission directly into the event day. Tree planting, environmental programming, vendor zone management, and overall time management across the venue are his responsibility. By the time a student who planted a tree with us is sitting their final exams, that tree will already be standing.

Head of Security
Mark Davinci

Mark's job is to make sure nothing on event day disrupts the experience for the students, the crew or the partners. He works closely with school scouts, prefects and clubs to manage access, protect equipment, and handle crowd dynamics with precision. His presence at an event means everyone else can focus entirely on their role.

Section four
The core team.

Dancers, MCs, sound engineers, videographers, camera operators, performers, hypemen - the people whose work is felt in every second of the event, even when they are not the one on the mic.

Dance Crew Lead
Khloe Nyarangi

Khloe leads five dancers and is the person who warms the crowd up long before the main programme begins. She runs student dance workshops during the day, supports the modelling runway, and has a specific gift for making students who have never been on a stage feel like they belong on one.

Sound Engineer
Pauline Masika

Pauline handles all video and audio recording with the kind of technical attention that separates good content from great content. She sets up lapel mics, manages interview audio, and leads post-production on the content that goes to PPP TV. She is the reason the broadcasts sound the way they do.

Actor · Model · MC Support
Fred Otieno

Fred holds multiple roles in the production and does each one without drawing attention to the fact that he is doing three things at once. He supports backstage logistics, manages model lineups, and works alongside Paps when the programme needs an extra pair of hands on stage.

K
Videographer · Creative
Khalif Arts
Innocent Kahanja

Khalif is the one with the camera at the angles everyone else missed. He covers the event floor, captures the experience from inside the crowd, and brings a creative eye to content that makes the social reels feel like they were made by someone who cared. Works closely with Rania to ensure no moment leaves the building without being recorded properly.

J
CEO · Ashton Sounds

Jeff Ashton is the CEO of Ashton Sounds - the official stage audio partner of Urban Gang Tour. Every PA system, every microphone, every monitor mix at every event runs through his team. The reason the stage does not just look good but sounds the part.

DJ Partner
