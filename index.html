What this is

Three pillars.
One unforgettable day.

We walk into a school or campus with a full professional crew. A proper stage. DJs, hype, cameras, energy. Then we hand the mic to the students and let them show the country who they are.

01
Compete.
Ten battle lanes. Singing, rap, dance, spoken word, poetry, modelling, news reporting, public speaking, art, even a teachers' competition. Real winners. Real certificates. A TV moment that belongs to them.
02
Connect.
Aspiring journalists shadow our camera crew. Future stylists work the runway with our modelling team. Young presenters share the stage with Eugine and Lucy. The career you dream about is standing in front of you for a day.
03
Celebrate.
From the first DJ set to the last award, this day belongs to your students. Tree planting. Group photos. Urban Gang Moments instant prints. Snacks. Real conversations. Smiles shared with people who actually care.
04
Broadcast.
Then we film it all and air it on national television. Urban News on PPP TV Kenya, with reach up to 8 million viewers. The school that hosts us becomes the school the country watched.

One day, explained

A professional crew.
National television.
A student who will
never forget it.

Eugine Micah and Lucy Ogunde steer every minute. From the first DJ set to the last award given out. That is not production value. That is identity.

Morning
The day begins
Stage up. PA running. Cameras rolling. Urban Gang Snacks open. By assembly bell, the venue is already transformed.
Mid-morning
The pods. The pairing.
Mentorship sessions with working media, finance, digital, and modelling professionals. Students who want those careers shadow our crew live.
Midday
