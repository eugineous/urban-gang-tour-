# Urban Gang Tour: positioning, page purposes and repair order

18 October 2026 · Nairobi. This is a source-led working brief, not a screenshot-based visual audit, proof of every business fact, or approval to publish every proposed word. It distinguishes code changes in this branch from recommendations and unverified production behaviour. The existing visual language remains flexible: no compulsory palette, font or sitewide aesthetic is prescribed.

## The actual problem

The website is not short of pages. It has accumulated overlapping promises and separate sources of truth. A visitor has to work out whether they are dealing with an event team, an events directory, a media portfolio, a ticketing platform or a merchandise brand before they can act.

Seven concrete issues explain that impression:

1. **Audience and service are not explicit enough at the entry point.** The homepage leads with the company name and a broad purpose slogan. Its 2022 archive link competes with the current business proposition. That can confuse a first-time school representative or event organiser; the extent of confusion needs user testing, not our taste.
2. **Navigation reflects accumulated sections rather than customer questions.** “Experience,” “Tour stops” and “Portfolio” require interpretation. Services, upcoming events, delivered work and shopping are different tasks. Plain labels make their destinations easier to predict.
3. **Merchandise discovery was split.** Fourteen live products were returned in the earlier production read; 24 proposed designs existed in a separate lookbook. A customer should not have to discover that architecture. These are different commercial states, not reasons for separate entry journeys.
4. **Some public history was disconnected from current event state.** Portfolio, About and Proof used a static school timeline, while the event listing used the database. A status correction could update one surface but leave another claiming completion. The branch now shares published event records for those completed-school sections.
5. **Validation discards useful context.** A missing caption under one uploaded poster collapsed the entire partner object into a generic error. That is a validation/reporting defect, not evidence that the code is “too long” or needs an AI agent behind the form.
6. **A successful order request and a successful payment are separate facts.** Receipts, PDFs, payment status and account orders already exist. The previous audit reproduced initiation retry defects; adding another receipt page would not repair them. There is no verified evidence here that a real customer was charged twice.
7. **Presentation and publication maturity are mixed.** A proposed design is not manufactured stock, a poster is not proof of delivery, an older founder appearance is not automatically a UGT contract, and an external seller is not UGT ticket inventory. The interface needs these distinctions without turning every page into a disclaimer.

The source does **not** show a redirect from `/portfolio` to `/events`: Portfolio is its own page and links individual projects to their event records. An observed incorrect click destination still needs a browser reproduction; do not “fix” it by deleting the portfolio.

## Positioning and identity hierarchy

Working proposition, grounded in the supplied event posters and current offering:

> Urban Gang Tour brings school and campus events to life through live hosting, entertainment and event experiences in Kenya.

This is a positioning hypothesis to validate with actual customers, not a claim that research has proved market fit.

- **Parent brand:** Urban Gang Tour. Explain the event/hosting offer before the list of projects.
- **Primary commissioning audiences:** adult school/institution representatives; campus and event organisers; organisations commissioning hosts, appearances or event promotion. Confirm relative commercial priority against actual enquiries and signed work.
- **Secondary audiences:** people checking events and admission, followers exploring the work, and merchandise buyers. They need direct routes, not the same booking pitch everywhere.
- **Merchandise:** a shop within Urban Gang Tour. Color Blast is a collection, not another competing company identity or an implied stock claim.
- **News and media:** stories and the hosts’ documented work support the brand; they do not need to make the homepage look like a separate news business.
- **Founders:** Eugine Micah and Lucy Ogunde have named profiles and clearly credited work. Their earlier career archive must be labelled separately from UGT-delivered events.
- **Partner work:** distinguish the organiser, contracting company, UGT role and actual ticket seller. Existing confirmation that Sherehe Vibes Africa contracted specified events does not prove it contracted every event in the archive.
- **Legal seller:** confirm the actual registered/trading identity, receipt details and policies from original records. Do not infer legal structure, incorporation, tax status, received payments or profits from chat memory.

## Navigation and homepage hierarchy

Primary navigation in this branch: **Services · Events · Our work · Shop · About**, with **Book an event** as the action and **Cart** as a utility. The expanded menu offers Upcoming events, All events and Past events explicitly, plus Photos, Videos, News, Partners, Orders & receipts and Contact. Existing URLs are retained; no mass route migration is needed.

Proposed homepage order, before any visual composition is chosen:

1. Who UGT helps and what someone can book; one dominant booking action.
2. Three concise service choices: school events; campus/live events; hosts and appearances. Each should lead to the relevant brief, not a generic media gallery.
3. Selected delivered work, with exact credited role and approved supporting material. Do not lead with a rotating wall of every logo ever associated with a founder.
4. Next confirmed events, with current status and correct admission/seller.
5. A compact merchandise selection linking to the complete shop.
6. Eugine and Lucy, one short introduction each and real profile links.
7. A short enquiry invitation and useful footer. No campaign interruption while someone is entering a form or paying.

This defines content priorities, not a compulsory visual identity. A later visual pass should explore composition using real approved media, not invent new faces, client claims or generic stock scenes.

## Proposed customer-facing copy

Drafts for approval; existing admin-managed copy has not been overwritten.

**Homepage**

Headline: “School and campus events. Live hosting. Urban Gang Tour.”

Introduction: “Planning an event for your school, campus or organisation? Tell us about your audience. We’ll discuss the programme, hosts and what your event needs.”

Actions: “Plan an event” · “See our work”

**Services**

Headline: “What can we help you organise?”

School events: “Talent shows, cultural days, colour experiences and live hosting, planned with the institution.”

Campus and live events: “Hosting and entertainment for student events, celebrations and live stages. We agree our role and scope with the organiser.”

Hosts and appearances: “Enquire about Eugine Micah or Lucy Ogunde for hosting, presenting and interviews. Share the date, audience and format.”

Action: “Share your event brief”

**Events**

Headline: “What’s coming up.”

Introduction: “Find event dates, venues and admission details. Each listing names the organiser and where to get tickets, when available.”

Filters: “Upcoming” · “Past events” · “All events”

**Our work**

Headline: “Our work.”

Introduction: “Explore published Urban Gang Tour event records and the founders’ earlier stage and media archive.”

Case-record labels: “Organiser” · “Our role” · “Credited team” · “Event date” · “Photos and film”. An upcoming booking is not a delivered case study.

**Shop**

Headline: “Shop.”

Introduction: “All merchandise in one place. Browse live products and Color Blast designs.”

States: “Add to cart” for approved live products; “Out of stock” when tracked stock is zero; “Design · not on sale” and “Proposed KES …” for unapproved concepts. Do not describe a studio mockup as a product photograph or enable payment from a design bag.

**About and profiles**

About: “Urban Gang Tour works with schools, campuses and event organisers on live hosting and entertainment experiences. Meet the people behind the tour and explore the work.”

Eugine: “Eugine Micah — event host and presenter. Explore his stage and presenting work, or enquire about an appearance.”

Lucy: “Lucy Ogunde — presenter and interviewer. Explore her on-camera and event work, or enquire about an appearance.”

These concise introductions do not replace verified biographies. Confirm titles, current affiliations and permissions before extending them. Do not invent awards, years of experience, audience figures, body measurements or employment status.

**Enquiry and payment feedback**

Enquiry: “Your request is saved. Reference: … Sending an enquiry does not reserve the date.”

Uncertain payment: “We are checking your payment. Please do not pay again while this order is being checked.”

Confirmed payment: “Payment confirmed. Open your receipt and downloads.”

Poster error: “Poster 2: add a short caption before publishing. Your upload is kept. You can save this event privately as a draft.”

## Research applied, not borrowed branding

- Clear labels should predict the destination; generic/branded terms can hide relevant content. That supports Services, Our work and explicit event history links. [Nielsen Norman Group: Information Scent](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/information-scent/).
- An explicit View All route helps mobile shoppers reach the full product list. That supports one complete shop rather than forcing collection gateways. It does not justify pretending concepts are purchasable. [Baymard: View All in Mobile Navigation](https://baymard.com/research-articles/mobile-main-nav-view-all).
- Errors should identify the affected control, give a correction and support jumping to it. That informed poster-specific targets, inline caption errors and retained entries. This is not a WCAG compliance certificate. [W3C WAI: User Notifications](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/notifications/).
- Product metadata must describe the actual product and distinguish purchasable listings from non-purchase pages. Existing canonical/OG/Product markup is present; it does not guarantee social distribution, search ranking or eligibility. [Google Search Central: Product Structured Data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product).
- Performance targets should be measured in field data: at the 75th percentile, LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1. No field/device result was produced in this pass. [web.dev: Core Web Vitals thresholds](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds).

## What is implemented, what is not

Implemented locally: precise shared poster validation; private unfinished poster drafts; safe same-site image/story URL formatting; one combined shop discovery grid; live-over-design precedence; product/design sharing controls; task-based header/footer/menu labels; current event data for completed-school sections on Portfolio/About/Proof; explicit payment progress and terminal-state wording. Existing artwork, models, media, secure login and financial records are preserved.

Not implemented or proved: a new visual design; physical Android/responsive inspection; Gallery hydration root-cause correction; full authenticated publishing round trips; durable M-Pesa/card initiation idempotency; atomic ticket reservations; enquiry/Gmail recovery repairs; approval of disputed event dates, legal identity, affiliations or media rights; product-specific social images; live sandbox settlement, document delivery, load capacity or guaranteed search/social indexing. All are still open; this branch must not be described as a complete platform reconstruction.

## Next repair and acceptance gates

1. **Money first:** execute the existing bounded durable M-Pesa initiation brief, then handle card request identity and atomic ticket reservations separately. Use isolated PostgreSQL concurrency/crash tests and separately authorised provider sandbox evidence. Never use real customers as a test or replay an uncertain provider request blindly.
2. **Content truth:** resolve the event-date discrepancy and approve factual roles/credits. A dated organiser confirmation, delivery record or permission is stronger than a compiled chat memory. Keep the financial ledger private and distinguish quotes, amounts due and actual receipts.
3. **Task verification:** representative school contacts, organisers and buyers should each identify the relevant route and complete their task without coaching. Record wrong turns, completion and recovery failures. No universal design preference or “ADHD-friendly” claim substitutes for this evidence.
4. **Visual/mobile pass:** when the supported capture tools and required Product Design references are accessible, capture existing states and test at narrow Android, common phone, tablet and desktop sizes. Check video framing, reflow, zoom, focus, keyboard/screen-reader behaviour and reduced motion. Then select a visual direction; do not impose one by default.
5. **Release:** freeze source, pass tests/typecheck/Cloudflare build/dry bundle, review the branch, then obtain the release gate before merging/deploying. No production publication is authorized by this document. Keep a compatible recovery path without deleting financial history or restoring unsafe writers.
