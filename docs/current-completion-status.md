   established. Direct Daraja/Pesapal must not be enabled as a substitute.

   ledger writes and paid end-to-end receipt delivery need authentic isolated
   merchant test evidence. No live charge, STK, refund or customer message was
   used as a test. Public catalogue reads alone cannot certify these.

   The earlier no-logo request is only partially met: an interior mark remains
   visible in a phone poster frame. A reviewed clean owned excerpt or localized
   treatment remains necessary; whole-video blur must not return.

   sitemap and robots fixes do not establish Bing/Brave/Baidu/Petal rankings;
   managed crawler restrictions and search-console ownership need separate proof.

   without useful diagnostics, and21st automated review lacks account credits.
   The approved release path uses verified local build/browser evidence and
   normal merge plus Cloudflare keep-vars deployment; none of those external
   checks is represented as green.
7. Admin Orders and customer fulfillment currently refresh on entry/actions or
   manual Refresh. Payment-result and email-delivery views have bounded polling,
   but automatic synchronization across three Orders/tracking views is unfinished.

   claiming immediate communication across every operational screen.
