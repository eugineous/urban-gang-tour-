WHO does not certify this application or UGT. |
| [Food Standards Agency: Providing food at community and charity events](https://www.food.gov.uk/safety-hygiene/providing-food-at-community-and-charity-events) — redirects to [GOV.UK publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/providing-food-at-community-and-charity-events), HTTP 200 | The official summary covers registration, food hygiene and allergens. | [Nairobi City County: How to protect food from contamination during distribution and marketing](https://nairobi.go.ke/protect-food-contamination-distribution-marketing) — HTTP 200, title confirmed | County guidance discusses hygienic handling, suitable equipment, temperature management, healthy personnel, relevant licensing and medical certificates in its food-processing discussion. It states a six-month certificate interval in that context. | The application asks for the status of applicable permits and food-handler certificates, with pending items recorded. It does not prescribe a universal expiry interval, issue a permit, collect medical records or replace county/venue confirmation. |

The service response is a JavaScript application shell. | This verifies the official service entrypoint, not its authenticated requirements, current fees, turnaround or an individual applicant's approval. No login or application submission was attempted. |

Opportunity names and deliverables are UGT discussion options, not ICC-prescribed packages or certification. The school alcohol/tobacco restriction remains an explicit UGT boundary. |
| [AMEC Integrated Evaluation Framework](https://amecorg.com/amecframework/) — HTTP 200, title confirmed | The framework describes aligning objectives, benchmarks, plans and targets, then measuring outputs, out-takes, outcomes and impact. It warns that activity alone does not establish meaningful outcomes. | The brief separates delivery outputs from outcomes and asks for agreed methods, evidence, limits and a reporting date. It promises no attendance, reach, sales or measurement result. |
